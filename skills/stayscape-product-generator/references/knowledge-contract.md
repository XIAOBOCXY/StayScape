# Travel knowledge contract

Travel knowledge is a public-reference layer, never inventory.

- ACTIVE records may be described with their supplied source and fields.
- VERIFY_REQUIRED or STALE records must retain 信息需确认 and may not be
  presented as confirmed opening, ticket, reservation, traffic, or availability
  facts.
- PUBLIC_REFERENCE entries cannot appear in room_inventory_id,
  hotel_service_ids, partner_resource_ids, resource_quantities, price, cost,
  capacity, or formal inclusions.
- A public record may inspire a light route note only when its supplied source
  URL and verification boundary are preserved.
