# Visitor knowledge boundary

Public travel knowledge and strategy references can improve recommendation
reasoning, but they do not change hotel product truth.

- Use source URLs and verification fields as internal evidence. Do not append generic source lists, raw links, retrieval statuses, or verification badges to normal visitor recommendations. Mention a source in prose only when it directly supports the visitor's factual question.
- Never say a public venue is reserved, included, sold out, open at a precise time, or available unless the supplied product or verified public facts establish that claim.
- A visitor may choose only from the supplied hotel-published products. The matcher does not hold inventory, create products, or assemble bookable multi-day plans.
- Strategy references can shape the explanation of themes, pace, and fit. They cannot supply current prices, inventory, opening hours, addresses, reservation rules, tickets, or route times.
