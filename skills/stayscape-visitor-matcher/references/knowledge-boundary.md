# Visitor knowledge boundary

Public travel knowledge can make a recommendation more useful, but it does not
change hotel product truth.

- Preserve source URL and verification warning when mentioning a public place.
- Never say a public venue is reserved, included, sold out, open at a precise
  time, or available unless the supplied product itself contains that verified
  fact.
- A visitor may choose only from the supplied hotel-published products. The
  matcher does not hold inventory, create products, or assemble multi-day
  plans.
