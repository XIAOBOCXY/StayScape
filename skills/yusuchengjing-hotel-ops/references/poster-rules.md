# Poster rules

The poster has two layers:

1. A visual brief or generated, text-free image layer.
2. A program-rendered text layer from a validated product snapshot.

The text layer may contain only validated product name, short subtitle, three
to five formal inclusions, validated selling price, real sellable quantity,
target crowd, and CTA. It must not invent room views, a pool, restaurant,
children's play area, landmark access, ticket rights, partner branding, or
scarcity.

The visual direction follows the actual core experience, crowd, time, weather,
and hotel style. Do not default to West Lake, tea leaves, rain, blue-green
ancient-style illustration, hotel bed, or generic tourist photography.

If an image model is unavailable, return a full poster prompt and a
text-layout brief. Mark the result as a visual-plan fallback rather than a
generated image.
